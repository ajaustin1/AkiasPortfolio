About Me

I'm an administrative and operations professional with more than six years of experience supporting senior leadership at NASA's Marshall Space Flight Center through federal contracting. My work sits at the intersection of executive operations, human resources, and organizational strategy — handling the complex, high-stakes work that keeps large federal programs on track.

In a dual-capacity role, I've overseen everything from multi-executive calendar management and CAITS action tracking to staffing plan maintenance and employee recognition programs. I partner directly with HR to resolve personnel matters, develop internal procedures, and coordinate initiatives across six organizations simultaneously.

I hold a BS in Commerce and Business Administration from the University of Alabama, and I'm currently pursuing my PHR certification through HRCI. I'm open to opportunities in federal HR, analyst roles, and corporate environments where operational rigor and people-centered leadership are valued.
